Type Shop

A store where the products are letters. Each shelf holds a different typeface, from blackletter to geometric sans. Grab letters with your real hands and spell words in the basket. Your webcam is only used for hand tracking, and the video never leaves your computer.

  1. Pinch (thumb + index) near a letter to grab it.
  2. Move your hand to carry it; open the pinch to drop or gently toss it.
  3. Move your hand toward the screen to reach deeper into the aisle.
  4. Drop letters into your shopping cart to spell a word, read left to right.
